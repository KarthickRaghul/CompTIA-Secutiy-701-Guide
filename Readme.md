I have cleared My Comptia Security+ 701 on 24/11/2026. There were three 76 questions in total and three PBQ. I scored 779/900.

### Exam Overview

- **Exam code:** SY0–701
- **Number of questions:** Up to 90
- **Question types:** Multiple-choice and performance-based questions (PBQs)
- **Time limit:** 90 minutes
- **Passing score:** 750 on a scale of 100 to 900
### PBQS

1. There were passwords of each user and their date of joining and last activity. They asked me to analyse the password and find the security flaws in them.(like password size, expiration, life, complexity...) after that they gave me a scenario that company was hacked and few details were leased and asked about what data is ok to be stay leaked and i was given a list of options. I choose the best one among the answers (like otp)
2. I was give a network system , Three IP address for R&D (192.168.x.x), Two Ip address for Engineers (10.10.x.x). and a firewall sits between them. I was given the network log of all 6 systems and were asked to identify whether the system is still affected / safe / malware spread from there. 
3. I was given two documents , two servers. Situation : Consider ourselves as soc analyst and some of the ips are causing issues, each likely to be malicious ips were given in two documents . There were only a commands (eg - netcat -o , ls -l) from which we could use and we need to identify and fix all the vulnerablitites in both servers. We had linux servers with root access but finding all the malicious links , identifying and terminating the processes with appropriate PID, finding the malicious file and removing them, analysing the log. This question was the difficult one.
